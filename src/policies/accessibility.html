Eternaluxe LLC is committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, including people with disabilities. We are working toward conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.

Our approach

We aim to provide keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus indicators, descriptive alternative text, sufficient color contrast, logical heading structure, labeled forms, reduced-motion support, and content that remains understandable without animation.

Need assistance?

If you experience difficulty accessing content or completing a purchase, email info@eternaluxe.net. Include the page address and a brief description of the issue. We will provide the information or service through an accessible alternative where reasonably possible.

Feedback

We welcome accessibility feedback. The production statement should include a verified response timeframe, the date of the most recent audit, and any known limitations still being addressed.
