Working draft. Eternaluxe must choose and document real cutoffs, remedies, subscription controls, and any minimum commitment. Counsel should review them against New York, federal, and other applicable consumer law before launch.

One-time arrangements

Fresh, custom, preserved, dried, and artificial arrangements may require different rules. The material type and refund eligibility should appear before purchase. Once sourcing, design, personalization, or dispatch has begun, a custom order may not be cancellable except where law requires or Eternaluxe agrees otherwise.

Damaged, incorrect, or undelivered orders

Customers should contact info@eternaluxe.net promptly with the order number, explanation, and photographs where relevant. After review, an appropriate remedy may include correction, replacement, redelivery, credit, or refund. This process does not limit non-waivable legal rights.

Floral Club renewal

Before enrollment, checkout must prominently disclose the amount, frequency, delivery charge, next billing date, automatic-renewal status, and cancellation method. An order confirmation should repeat those terms. Renewal consent and the policy version shown must be retained with the transaction record.

Pause, skip, or cancel

The final policy must state the exact deadline for pausing, skipping, or cancelling an upcoming arrangement and provide a simple online or email method. A request received before the cutoff stops the relevant future charge or delivery. A request received after design or sourcing has begun may apply only to later renewals, subject to applicable law and any remedy Eternaluxe offers.

Bespoke and event work

Bespoke commissions require a separate proposal stating deposits, payment milestones, design approvals, change orders, cancellation dates, nonrecoverable sourcing costs, installation, and removal. If that agreement conflicts with this general policy, the signed project agreement should control to the extent legally permitted.

Delivery failures

Remedies depend on responsibility for the failure. If Eternaluxe materially misses an accepted commitment, the customer should receive an appropriate correction or refund. Where incorrect customer information, inaccessible premises, or an unavailable recipient causes failure, disclosed redelivery or preparation costs may apply.

Jurisdiction note

This U.S./New York draft does not state that customers have a Colombian five-business-day withdrawal right. If Eternaluxe intentionally serves consumers in Colombia or another jurisdiction, local counsel should add the rights that actually apply to those customers.

How to request help

Email info@eternaluxe.net with the order number and requested resolution. The production policy should publish the response timeframe and verified business mailing address.
