# Homepage venue: three changing displays

The fixed original room remains the background for every frame. Three table arrangements and one wall installation appear in each of three 16-second chapters: ivory/moss, coral/floral relief, and plum-jewel/modular panels. Each layer fades in over 1.6–1.8 seconds; the completed display holds, then fades away before the next set. Total loop: 48 seconds. No additional homepage sections were added.

The generated sheets are separated into four transparent pieces and aligned reproducibly by `scripts/prepare-brand.mjs`. Original and mobile variants share registered coordinates. Pausing, offscreen/inactive-tab suspension, no-JS ivory fallback, and manual reduced-motion states remain. A failed set is skipped; other complete sets continue. The room remains visible even if every decorative image fails.

## Assets and prompts

Built-in image generation produced `assets/brand/venue-coral-sheet.png` and `assets/brand/venue-jewel-sheet.png`. These are AI-assisted conceptual additions to owner-authorized material, not photographs of completed installations.

### Coral sheet prompt

Use case: product-mockup. TRANSPARENT asset sheet, wide 2:1 canvas, divided into FOUR equal quadrants with absolutely transparent margins. Each quadrant contains exactly ONE independent photoreal floral object, NOT touching any other quadrant. No grid lines, no text, no background or furniture. Top-left: airy asymmetric blush/coral/burgundy wall-mounted floral relief, no frame, viewed slightly from right. Top-right: low bronze bowl with rich scarlet poppies, coral and orange ranunculus, airy stems. Bottom-left: petite blush calla arrangement in olive green ceramic vase. Bottom-right: petite orange ranunculus and delicate greenery in champagne vase. All four entirely visible, generous transparent gutter at least60px along central axes, natural warm side light from right matching reference room. Table vessels with subtle contact shadows directly beneath feet. Four objects clearly separated, nothing spills between quadrants. Intended for exact placement in supplied room.
